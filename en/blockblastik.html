About the game

>About the game Block Blitz is a block puzzle: drag a shape from the tray onto the 8x8 grid. Full rows and columns clear and score, and every clean placement raises the combo multiplier that fills the BLAST meter — when it is full the whole board is cleared. Six modes: classic, blitz, 2048, daily puzzle, treasure and challenge. What is inside Six game modes including daily puzzle Combo multiplier and BLAST meter Hammer, bomb and swap boosters 5x5, 6x6 and 8x8 board sizes Leaderboards and cloud save

How to play

  1. 1Drag one of the three pieces onto the 8×8 board — Tetris without falling blocks.
  2. 2A full row or column vanishes and scores.
  3. 3Filled lines charge the BLAST meter: it wipes the board and doubles the score.
  4. 4Pieces stay until they fit — think two moves ahead or the board jams.
