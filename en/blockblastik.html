Block Blitz — 8x8 block puzzle

Place shapes on an 8x8 board, complete rows and columns and watch them burst. Six modes, combo multipliers and a BLAST meter.

About the game

Block Blitz is a block puzzle: drag a shape from the tray onto the 8x8 grid. Full rows and columns clear and score, and every clean placement raises the combo multiplier that fills the BLAST meter — when it is full the whole board is cleared. Six modes: classic, blitz, 2048, daily puzzle, treasure and challenge.

What is inside

  • Combo multiplier and BLAST meter
  • Hammer, bomb and swap boosters
