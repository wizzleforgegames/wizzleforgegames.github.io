About the game

>About the game Watermelon 2048 is a one-finger merge puzzle. A fruit falls from the top, equal fruits fuse into the next one in the chain. Fill the board and the run ends. Earn coins for merges, spend them on boosters and backgrounds, chase the daily challenge and the endless zen mode. What is inside One-finger control — drag and release Combo chains and bonus merges Daily run, zen mode and quests Levels, coins and achievements Local high scores, works offline

How to play

  1. 1Hold your finger (or mouse) over the field and move to aim, release to drop the fruit.
  2. 2Two identical fruits merge into the next bigger one. Cherry → … → watermelon.
  3. 3Don't let the stack reach the top line — that ends the run.
  4. 4Chains trigger COMBO, and boosters (hammer, bomb) clear the crowd.
