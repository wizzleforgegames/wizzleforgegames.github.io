Watermelon 2048: Merge Fruits

A fruit merge arcade: drop a fruit, join two equal ones and grow all the way to a watermelon. Combos, daily quests, zen mode and records.

About the game

Watermelon 2048 is a one-finger merge puzzle. A fruit falls from the top, equal fruits fuse into the next one in the chain. Fill the board and the run ends. Earn coins for merges, spend them on boosters and backgrounds, chase the daily challenge and the endless zen mode.

What is inside

  • One-finger control — drag and release
  • Combo chains and bonus merges
  • Daily run, zen mode and quests
  • Levels, coins and achievements
  • Local high scores, works offline

FAQ

Do I need to install the game?

No — it runs in the browser.

Да, игра работает без интернета, рекорды хранятся на устройстве.
