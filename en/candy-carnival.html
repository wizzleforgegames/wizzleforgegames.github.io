How to play

  1. 1Swap two adjacent candies to line up three or more.
  2. 2Four in a row makes a rocket, five a bomb, an L-shape a rainbow — they clear the board.
  3. 3Every level has its goal: collect a colour, break ice, deliver orders.
  4. 4200 levels and three modes; if you get stuck, hammer and shuffle save the day.
