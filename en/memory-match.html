About the game

Photo Pairs is a memory match game for kids and adults. Turn cards face up and find equal pairs. 22 photo decks (animals, ocean, space, food, vehicles, dinosaurs and more) with four difficulty levels each — 88 levels in total. Three modes: classic, combo and timed blitz. Stars depend on your speed, hints are limited.
