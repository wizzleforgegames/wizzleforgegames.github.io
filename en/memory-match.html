About the game

>About the game Photo Pairs is a memory match game for kids and adults. Turn cards face up and find equal pairs. 22 photo decks (animals, ocean, space, food, vehicles, dinosaurs and more) with four difficulty levels each — 88 levels in total. Three modes: classic, combo and timed blitz. Stars depend on your speed, hints are limited. How to play

  1. 1Flip two cards: a match stays face up.

  4. 4Three modes — Classic, Combo and Time — plus a hint when you stall.
