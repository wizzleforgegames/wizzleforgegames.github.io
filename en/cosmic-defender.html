About the game

Cosmic Defender is a fast vertical shooter. Fly, shoot and dodge: waves of enemies, meteor belts and bosses between levels. Collect power-ups, upgrade weapons and shields, unlock nine ships with different stats. Revive with a rewarded video or keep your gold with double reward.

What is inside

  • Nine ships with unique weapons
  • Weapon, shield and drone upgrades
  • Boss fights and endless waves
  • Rewarded revive and double reward
  • Works on phone and desktop

How to play

  1. Steer the ship with finger or mouse — the guns fire automatically.
  2. Dodge the swarm and meteors, grab power-ups and gold.
  3. Each wave ends with a boss: keep your distance and hit the weak spot.
  4. Between waves upgrade the ship and weapons — the swarm becomes beatable.
