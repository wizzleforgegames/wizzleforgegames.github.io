How to play

  1. 1Steer the ship with finger or mouse — the guns fire automatically.
  2. 2Dodge the swarm and meteors, grab power-ups and gold.
  3. 3Each wave ends with a boss: keep your distance and hit the weak spot.
  4. 4Between waves upgrade the ship and weapons — the swarm becomes beatable.
