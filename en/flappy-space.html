How to play

  1. 1One tap — a short upward burst. Stop tapping and the ship falls.
  2. 2Fly through gaps between asteroids and planets without touching them.
  3. 3Collect crystals and rescue the crew: rescues unlock new ships in the hangar.
  4. 431 missions and a daily reward: come back every day to keep the bonus.
